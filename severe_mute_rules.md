# 🔇 Mute Punishments (Severe Cases)

⚠️ Always post punishments **with evidence** in the punishments channel.

---

### 💢 Severe Toxicity / Hate Speech
- Racism, sexism, or extreme insults (not slang/slurs)  
- Usually targets multiple people  

→ **Punishment:**  
- 7-day mute  
- Repeat → punishment doubles  

---

### ⚠️ Threats & Severe Bullying
- Threats of harm or serious harassment  

→ **Punishment:**  
- 7-day mute  
- Repeat → punishment doubles  

---

### 🔞 Explicit Content
- Sharing NSFW content or links  

→ **Punishment:**  
- 7-day mute  
- Repeat → punishment doubles  

---

### 🔁 Mute Evasion
Using alt accounts to bypass a **serious mute**  

→ **Punishment:**  
- Permanent mute (9999) on the alt account
