# 🔈 Mute Punishments (Mild Cases)

⚠️ Always **warn first**  
- Log every warning and mute in the punishments channel (with evidence)

💡 Note: Many cases are lighter now → players should use the **block feature** when possible

---
### Continuous Slurs Usage 
This must have MULTIPLE evidence of being targeted towards specific players or in multiple occasions publicly.
Context matters A LOT. Always check chatlogs if unsure to retrieve the context and don't rely only on single occasions/single reports without said context.

1. Warning  
2. 1 day mute  
3. 2 day mute  
4. 3 day mute

All the next mutes after the 4th offense will be kept at 3-day mute.

---

### 📢 Spamming
Repeated spam messages (obviously serious spamming)

**Punishments:**
1. Warning  
2. 1 day mute  
3. 2 day mute
4. 3 day mute

All the next mutes after the 3rd offense will have a 3 days length.

---

### 🎭 Staff Impersonation
Pretending to be staff in order to **gain advantages** or to steal accounts. Use a higher duration for serious cases like trying to steal accounts.

**Punishments:**
1. Warning  
2. 1–3 day mute  
3. 7 day mute  
4. 14 day mute  
5. 28 day mute  
6. 90 day mute  

---

### 📰 Fake Information
Spreading harmful or false claims (serious false claims or seriously harmful claims)

**Punishments:**
1. Warning  
2. 1–4 day mute  
3. 7 day ban  
4. 30 day ban  
5. 60 day ban  
6. 90 day ban  
7. Permanent ban
