# 👢 Kick Punishments

⚠️ **Always warn first**  
- Do NOT kick without warning

⚠️ Kicks must have:
- Clear **reason**
- **Evidence**

---

## ✅ Valid Reasons to Kick
- Player is abusing bugs/glitches for advantage  
- Player avoids votekick by leaving and rejoining  

---

## ❌ Invalid Reasons to Kick
- Personal dislike  
- Other players asking you to kick  

👉 Use the **votekick system** instead

---

## 🏷️ Room Title Rules

### 🔗 Bad / Suspicious Titles
- Fake links (OnlyFans, scams, cheats)  
- Extremely offensive titles (e.g. severe hate speech)

**Punishments:**
1. Warning + change title  
2. Disable room creation (1–7 days) + change title  

👉 For **serious links (scam/cheats)** → you can skip warning and disable room creation immediately

---

## 🌾 Farming Rules
(Farming EXP, kills, AFK, etc.)

**Punishments:**
1. Warning  
2. 7-day room creation ban + **alt accounts banned permanently**  
3. 7-day account ban + mission reset + **alt accounts banned permanently**  

👉 If repeated often → discuss higher punishment with staff

---

## 🗳️ Votekick Abuse
- Targeting a player across multiple rooms  
- Must have **clear proof of stalking**

**Punishment:**
- Disable votekick (1–14 days)  
- Repeat → doubled  

---

## 🐞 Bugs & Glitches

### Normal Rooms
- No staff punishment → use votekick  

### Clan War Rooms
- 1-day ban  
- Repeat → doubled  

---

## 📩 Report Abuse
Fake or misleading reports  

**Punishments:**
1. Warning  
2. 1-day ban  

---

## 👥 Account Sharing
- Not punished directly  
- BUT all actions apply as if **one owner only**

---

## 📝 Reminder
Always include in punishment logs:
- Evidence  
- Player name  
- Reason  
- Punishment given
